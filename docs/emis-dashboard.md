# EMIS dashboard · Tejasvi Campus 3.8.0

Open your school or college in **EMIS → Dashboard**. The homepage is specific to your role and institution. The selected academic year applies to year-based records; the program and staff directories remain institution-wide as before.

| Role | What appears on Overview |
| --- | --- |
| Super Admin and Admin | Attendance, applications, fee receipts, operational follow-ups and program-level enrollment, teaching and attendance |
| Faculty | Attendance in assigned courses, the weekly schedule, published results and teaching assignments |
| Student | Personal attendance and published results, a schedule and courses for the enrolled program and semester |
| Finance Head | Fee receipts, invoices due, expenditure, open follow-ups and an invoice table with amounts paid and outstanding |
| Other staff | Charts, registers and actions appropriate to their existing EMIS permissions |

Use the selector beside the menu button at the top to switch between **Overview**, **Reports & details** (or **My records**) and **School/College information**. Super Admin still has access to the complete reporting and institutional views. Open a chart’s footer to see the underlying register. Focus a bar and use the arrow keys to read its exact value. Long tables have sorting and page controls; on a phone each row keeps its column labels.

Attendance is present plus late divided by present, late and absent marks. An excused-only or unmarked period has no attendance percentage. Fee collections use actual receipts. The finance role’s Invoiced fees card groups invoice face values by due date; the invoice table shows outstanding balances. Expenditure uses posted expense vouchers. All financial amounts are in NPR. Current-year trend cards look back from today in Kathmandu; for an earlier academic year they end at the latest recorded date for that metric. A table’s attendance column covers the selected year. Academic role scopes are enforced by the existing server before the dashboard receives records.

The illustration is an original asset for Tejasvi. Visual reference: Creative Tim’s Material Dashboard Shadcn, [as distributed by ThemeWagon](https://themewagon.com/themes/material-shadcn/). The EMIS charts, permissions, register links and dashboard components are Tejasvi implementations.

The separately distributed interactive preview uses fictional records and supports read-only exploration. It does not connect to a college database or seed an installation.
