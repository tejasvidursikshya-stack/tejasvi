# Creator collection artwork

Original project assets generated with the built-in image generator. The transparent objects and full-bleed landscape are composited as independent DOM layers; imagery contains no interface text. Local WebP renditions live in `public/assets/workshop/`.

## create.webp

Use case: product-mockup. Original Tejasvi Creator Studio gallery hero cutout to be dynamically composited into a website; no text or UI. True transparent background with entirely empty corners, a narrow soft contact shadow if possible, no floor, no environment, no rectangular backdrop. Landscape 3:2 composition. 3/4 product photograph viewed slightly from above, subject centered; silhouette fills 78 percent of width leaving generous margins. Hand-crafted paper textures, matte navy and saturated lapis enamel, pale ivory and a touch of amber brass, precise warm studio rim light from the upper left. High-end physical objects, not a flat vector, no generic neon AI icons, no lettering, no logos, no text, no watermark. Subject: A deluxe open ivory art book dramatically flaring into a little kinetic solar system: navy blue endpapers, a copper-orange sun, several rich cobalt or amber spheres in fine brass orbital rings emerging from the fold, one turned paper page midair. A clear, joyful sculptural silhouette.

## horizon.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Generate a panoramic wide 16:9 landscape background plate for an exquisite art-directed educational product showcase in Nepal. A minimalist tactile museum diorama of terraced Himalayan foothills made of layered, undulating ivory and champagne paper; far hazy pale peaks, large airy cloud forms made of matte ivory fiber high on the right in a glowing beige sky. Distant hills cut across the lower quarter, like an elegant horizon in a luxury product campaign. Bare foreground floor, soft diffuse sunlight from upper left. Monochromatic warm champagne, tawny cream, very pale clay: no green. Spacious, dreamlike but photographic; fine felt and handmade paper texture, graceful organic shapes. No teaching object, no books, no people, no roads, no car, no text, no overlay, no logo. The full middle foreground will be occupied by a separate product. Wide horizontal composition.
