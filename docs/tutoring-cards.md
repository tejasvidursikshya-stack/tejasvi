# Teach and practise with cards

**Teachers and institution administrators:** In Learning, open **Teaching & content → Tutoring cards → New deck**. The library shows the decks you authored in the current institution. There is no AI key or paid generation step.

| Choose | Front | Reverse | Audience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cue cards | Speaking cue | Talking points | Teacher only; use Rehearse during a tutorial |
| Flashcards | Recall question | Answer | Private until you share the saved deck with a course |

1. Name the deck and choose a card colour. Write both sides of the first card. **Show front/back** changes the design preview.
2. Choose **Add another card**. Use the numbered buttons to move between cards. **Private teacher note & card actions** lets you add a note, move, duplicate or remove the selected card. A deck holds up to 40 cards.
3. **Save deck** writes to the institution’s MySQL database. **Rehearse** shows one card at a time. **Large text** makes both sides easier to read when projecting or practising. A private note stays hidden unless you reveal it. When sharing your screen with a class, leave it hidden.
4. For flashcards, choose a **Published course** under **Share with learners**, then **Share flashcards**. If no courses appear, publish a course or ask an administrator to assign you one. For changes, save your edits, select the course again and choose **Update course copy**. **Withdraw** removes that course’s copy without deleting your source.
5. **Students:** Open an assigned course, find **Practice flashcards** and choose **Practice**. Answer from memory, reveal the answer, and select **Got it** or **Practice again**. At the end you can repeat just the marked cards. A practice round lasts for the current visit; it does not affect grades.

A teacher’s optional notes and all cue cards stay private. Changes saved in the editor never silently change the learner copy. Withdrawal stops subsequent reads; it does not erase a learner’s already-open screen. A shared deck must be withdrawn from its courses before you can delete it.
