TEJASVI CAMPUS 3.4 — CREATOR STUDIO & LMS DASHBOARD

QUICK LOOK
Open the separate Tejasvi-Campus-Homepage-Preview.html in a desktop browser.
No installation is required. Click Product demo for the dashboard and edition
chart, or AI Creator Studio for the animated collection and worked examples.

UPDATE ON A MAC (CURRENT CAMPUS 3.0, 3.1, 3.2 OR 3.3)
1. Stop the running Campus Terminal with Control+C. Leave MySQL on and keep
   your normal backup.
2. Unzip Tejasvi-Campus-3.4.0.zip. In the NEW Tejasvi-Campus-3.4 folder,
   double-click Update-Homepage.command.
3. Select the ORIGINAL configured Campus folder you normally run.
4. Open Start-Tejasvi.command in that ORIGINAL folder. After the rebuild,
   visit http://localhost:3000/ and refresh.
The updater checks for local source changes and backs up its edits. It
never copies settings or uploads. The next normal startup applies pending
migrations; 019 adds resource-sharing metadata to the existing studio
draft table. No samples, default users or institutions are inserted.
New installation? Use Start-Tejasvi.command and follow START-HERE.txt.

ON THE HOMEPAGE
• In the demo, select an edition. The left dashboard lets you perform an
  office or training task and try the learner view. The right chart compares
  the editions using a visible checklist. No external vendor scores are used.
• In Creator Studio, choose Create, Build, Assist or Plan to change plates.
  Open a sample or select one of the six output formats. Try the quiz,
  turn flashcards, download slides or render a short silent lesson video.

IN THE LMS
1. Sign in with an authorized school/college administrator or teacher
   account, or as a corporate training administrator.
2. In the Learning workspace, open Workspace dashboard or AI Studio.
3. Choose a tool, enter a topic and audience, and add source notes. Start
   a writing draft works without an AI key. Generate AI kit requires an
   enabled organization, a configured provider and explicit confirmation.
4. In the review desk select a format, choose Edit this output, then Save
   edits. Export PPTX, printable activities/cards/storybooks or a silent
   video (WebM or MP4, according to browser support).
5. Import a reviewed draft for later publication, or use Publish reviewed
   course to create a new course with assigned access after review. Already
   imported materials can be shared/withdrawn from the studio. Learners
   open Shared learning materials inside a course they can access.
Source notes, facilitation notes and graded quiz answer keys are not part
of the learner resource pack. Corporate workspaces do not include EMIS.

See docs/creator-studio.md for access, setup, formats and maintenance.
